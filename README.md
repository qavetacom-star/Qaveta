## An independent trading technology studio

Qaveta develops tools for traders who want a more practical way to automate strategies and manage their trading environment. Our work brings together MT5 Expert Advisors, AI-assisted strategy research, cloud desktops, and custom EA development.

We treat each strategy as a product with its own approach, operating requirements, and risk profile. Our lineup evolves as we develop and release new EAs, rather than fitting every product into a fixed strategy category.

## What we do

| Area | Our focus |
| --- | --- |
| **MT5 Expert Advisors** | Original EA products, led by our flagship Qaveta AI, alongside traditional and hybrid strategy designs. |
| **Cloud desktops** | Forex-focused and general-purpose cloud desktops for running trading terminals and managing remote workspaces. |
| **Custom EA development** | Turning a defined trading idea into an implementation, including strategy logic, interfaces, and licensing integration. |
| **Brand partnerships** | EA branding and OEM services, with additional platform delivery options according to the agreed scope. |

## Qaveta Desktop

A compact Windows companion for your Qaveta account:

- Manage your cloud desktops and open remote sessions in dedicated windows.
- Arrange multiple sessions in split-screen layouts and keep machines identifiable with personal notes.
- View EA licenses, account balance, announcements, and support requests.
- Open the Client Center for purchases, top-ups, and renewals.
- Use the interface in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Russian, or French.
